DATA & METHODOLOGY

A foundation for better decisions.

This frontend preview demonstrates how Sri Lanka’s bank networks can be explored and compared.

All branch records are illustrative.

Names, counts, and coordinates are synthetic sample data near town centres. They do not represent verified branch locations, national network totals, or recommendations.

What the figures mean

Branch counts and district counts are calculated from the currently filtered sample records. A marker indicates a sample point; it does not establish the bank’s service coverage.

Map sources

The local country outline is from Natural Earth. Street tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap contributors and require an internet connection. Branch data stays in your browser.

Ready for verified data

The next dataset can provide bank, branch name, district, latitude, longitude, and existing or proposed status. No backend is required.
