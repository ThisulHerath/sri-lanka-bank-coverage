DATA & METHODOLOGY

Published records. Clear provenance.

Snapshot collected on 13 November 2026. Counts describe the imported directories, rather than a certified current national network total.

Reported district network · 2025

CBSL Economic and Social Statistics 2026, Table 7.8, reports 274 Commercial Bank, 672 BOC and 748 People’s Bank outlets across 25 districts (1,694 combined). Figures are provisional. The source defines the scope as “All Banking outlets except Student Savings Units”. These aggregates do not contain individual addresses or coordinates.

A facility badge means the source lists that facility at the location. It is not the number of individual machines. Missing facility information means unknown availability.

Locations and district assignments

Pins retain coordinates published in bank direction links, People’s Bank’s official map locator, or referenced Google Maps place listings. These have not been independently surveyed. Missing coordinates and out-of-country or conflicting coordinates are withheld. Address search links open Google Maps searches and do not establish exact locations.

Districts are assigned from coordinates, source district fields, or a town reference. Some source fields and inferred assignments require review; each location shows its assignment method. Zero classified records is not evidence that a bank has no presence.

Geography and references

District boundaries: geoBoundaries LKA ADM2, OpenStreetMap / Wambacher, 2017 geography, ODbL 1.0. Town reference: SriLankaCitiesDatabase (MIT). Town coordinates are never used as branch pins.

This application reads local static data. Source and Google Maps links require internet access. Street and road tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap and require internet access. No backend or paid map API is required.
